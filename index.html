5:30 AM - The Trailhead

The alarm went off before the sun came up. The parking lot was empty and the air was cold. From here the summit looked really far away, but the plan was simple: get to the top before sunset.

Into the Trees

12:00 PM - The Forest

By noon the trail went straight into the forest. It was quiet except for birds and my own breathing. The switchbacks kept going and going, and every time I thought I was close there was another one.

The Summit

7:15 PM - The Top

Made it just in time. The sun dropped behind the mountains and the whole sky turned orange and purple. Every sore muscle was worth it for this view.

Goodnight

10:00 PM - Camp

Set up the tent on a flat spot below the peak and watched the stars come out. Tomorrow we go back down.
